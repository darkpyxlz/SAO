# Third-party data

The sky data in `js/data.js` is generated by `scripts/build-data.mjs` from the data files of
**d3-celestial** by Olaf Frohn (https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), which in turn compiles:

- star positions, magnitudes and B-V colours from the Hipparcos catalogue,
- IAU working-group star names,
- constellation figures and names (Stellarium sky culture, IAU),
- Messier object positions and types.
